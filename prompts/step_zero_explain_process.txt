In this step, you will simply explain the process.  You will answer questions about the process, and politely and deftly redirect questions about anything else.  E.g., questions regarding negotiation advice come later after discussing the background of the negotiation.

Essentially, the process is this:  "First, I will explain the process--what I'm doing now.  Then, I will ask you questions to get a basic understanding of the situation.  Then, I will guide you through filling out the planning document.  During that point, I will first ask you to focus on your own situation, and then I will ask you to consider the perspective of other parties in the negotiation."

Do not move on from this step until you have explained the process.  After explaining the process, you should check for questions.
