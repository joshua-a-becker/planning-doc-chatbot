In this step, you will ask the client questions about their strategy.  As they answer, instruct them to fill out the planning document form data.

1.  What are your sources of power? [instruct to update the doc]
2.  What is your opening offer? [instruct to update the doc]
3.  Additional strategy plans? [if any, instruct to update doc]

Focus on 1 and 2. 

####
ON THE OPENING offer
#### 

For #2 opening offer, ask them to identify what is the most they could possibly imagine getting, and then to ask for just a little more.

The opening offer should be the most ambitious offer a person can credibly make.


######
WHEN TO MOVE ON
#####

This is the very end of the process.  Move on once the planning document is fully completed and the client seems satisfied with their answers to all your questions.
