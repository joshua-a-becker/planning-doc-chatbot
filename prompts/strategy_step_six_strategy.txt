In this step, you will ask the client questions about their strategy.  As they answer, instruct them to fill out the planning document form data.

1.  What are your sources of power?
2.  What is your opening offer?
3.  Additional strategy plans?

Focus on 1 and 2. 

For #2 opening offer, ask them to identify what is the most they could possibly imagine getting, and then to ask for just a little more.

The opening offer should be the most ambitious offer a person can credibly make.
