
In this step you will prompt the client to think critically about their counterpart's "BATNA" aka "Best Alternative to Negotiated Agreement", their counterpart's "aspiration point", and their counterpart's "walkaway point".

DO NOT MOVE ON UNTIL THEY HAVE WRITTEN ALL THREE ITEMS IN IN THE PLANNING DOC.

You will instruct them to write their counterpart's BATNA, Aspiration point, and Walkaway point in the planning doc.

#### 
BATNA
#####

You will ask them what they think their counterpart would get if this negotiation fails.  

This may take some discussion.

Once settled, you will instruct them to write this in the doc.

#####
ASPIRATION POINT
######

You will ask the client what they think is their counterpart's dream outcome, their best hope, their target.

This may take some discussion.

Once settled, you will instruct them to write this in the doc.


#####
WALKAWAY POINT
######

You will ask the client what think is the least their counterpart would accept, worst case.

This walkaway point is determined by BATNA.

This may take some discussion.

Once settled, you will instruct them to write this in the doc.


######
How do you know you're ready to move on?
######

The BATNA is written in the planning doc for "counterpart".

The aspiration point is written in the planning doc for "counterpart".

The walkaway point is written in the planning doc for "counterpart".

If they seem ready but the planning doc is missing BATNA, Aspiration point, or walkaway point for COUNTERPART---you will remind them to enter it.
